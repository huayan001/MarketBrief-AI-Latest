About / 关于

MarketBrief AI is research software for organizing public market data: quotes, charts, company context, watchlists, and AI-assisted summaries.

MarketBrief AI 是一款研究软件，用来整理公开市场数据，包括行情、图表、公司资料、自选股和 AI 辅助摘要。

It is not a broker, custodian, or investment adviser. 它不是券商、资金托管方或投资顾问。
