Customer and billing support

Sign in to MarketBrief AI and select Feedback in the top navigation. Choose “Pricing & plan” for subscription or billing requests. Support requests are linked to the authenticated account email so we can locate the correct subscription without asking for sensitive payment details.

Response information

Please include the issue, account email, relevant date, and any non-sensitive error message. Never send passwords, verification codes, or full payment-card details.
