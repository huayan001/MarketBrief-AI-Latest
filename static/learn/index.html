Learning center / 学习中心

MarketBrief AI organizes public quotes, charts, indicators, news, and AI summaries so you can review a symbol in one place.

MarketBrief AI 把公开行情、图表、指标、新闻和 AI 摘要整理到同一工作流，方便你自行复核一只标的。

  • Search a symbol, then read the data-health card before trusting the chart. 先看数据健康，再读图表。
  • Watchlist radar tracks indicator changes. It does not place orders. 自选雷达只记录指标变化，不会下单。
  • Pro adds higher limits and read-only trade review. Pro 提高额度，并提供只读交易复盘。
