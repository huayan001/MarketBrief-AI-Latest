Refund & Cancellation / 退款与取消政策

Effective: August 11, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 11 日

Cancellation

Pro renews monthly at US$19.99 until canceled. Customers can cancel at any time from Plan & usage → Manage subscription → Cancel subscription. Cancellation stops future renewals and Pro access remains available through the end of the current paid billing period.

Refund requests

If the service was materially unavailable, a duplicate charge occurred, or a charge was made after a confirmed cancellation, submit a billing request within 14 days of the charge. We will review the account and payment records and, where appropriate, issue a full or partial refund to the original payment method.

Except where required by law or described above, fees for a billing period already used are non-refundable. This does not limit any mandatory consumer rights.

How to request help

Sign in, open Feedback, select Pricing & plan, and include the account email, charge date, amount, and reason. Do not include full card details.

中文摘要

Pro 费用为每月 19.99 美元并自动续费。用户可随时在“套餐与用量 → 管理订阅 → 取消订阅”中取消；取消后不再续费，权益持续至当前已付周期结束。如发生重复扣款、确认取消后仍扣款或服务严重不可用，请在扣款后 14 天内通过站内“反馈—价格与套餐”提交申请。除法律另有要求或上述情况外，已使用计费周期的费用通常不予退还。
