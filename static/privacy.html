Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective: August 11, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 11 日

Information we process

We process account email addresses, authentication and security records, saved watchlists and reports, usage records, support messages, and subscription status. Payment details are collected and processed by our payment provider; MarketBrief AI does not store full card numbers.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide requested features, manage subscription access, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and improve reliability.

Service providers

We may use infrastructure, market-data, AI, email, analytics, and payment providers only as needed to operate the service. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and choices

Records are retained only as reasonably required for service delivery, security, billing, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Users may request access, correction, or deletion through the in-app feedback form.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.
