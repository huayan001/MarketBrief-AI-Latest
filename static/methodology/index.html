Methodology / 研究方法

Charts and indicators are calculated from the market-data provider’s historical bars. The data-health card shows the source name, exchange, last bar, and sample count so you can see when a symbol looks stale or mismatched.

图表和指标来自行情源的历史 K 线。数据健康卡片会显示来源名称、交易所、最后一根 K 线和样本数，便于发现过期或代码映射问题。

Confirmed cryptocurrencies such as BTC-USD stay marked healthy. Names that look like tokenized stocks can still show a mapping warning. 已确认的加密资产（例如 BTC-USD）不会被标成代码映射错误；名称像代币化股票的标的仍会提示核对代码。

Momentum scans and trade review read third-party market or execution history. They do not send orders. 强势雷达和交易复盘只读取行情或成交历史，不会发送订单。

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell. 本站内容不是买卖建议。
